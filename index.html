About

Applied AI, business systems, and technical delivery

I am an AI Project Lead and AI Engineer with 5+ years of combined experience across artificial intelligence, software development, business systems, machine learning, data analytics, applied research, and client-facing technical delivery.

At Genesis Group Inc. and Genesis AI Garage, I lead and support confidential AI and software engagements from requirements discovery and feasibility assessment through planning, implementation, testing, deployment support, documentation, and handover.

My work combines hands-on technical implementation with business and systems analysis. I clarify requirements, analyze workflows and data, support solution planning, coordinate delivery, validate technical outputs, prepare documentation, and communicate progress with clients and technical teams.
